How to play

  • Tap anywhere on the screen to make Santa fly upwards. Let go and he drops.
  • Steer him through the gaps between the brick chimneys and the icicles.
  • Every chimney you pass scores a point. The night gets faster the further you go.
  • Touch a chimney, an icicle or the ground and the game is over.

Common questions

There's no sound

Check that your iPhone's silent switch (or Action button) isn't set to silent, turn the volume up, and make sure the speaker button in the top-left corner of the game shows sound switched on.

What does "Keep going" do?

After a crash you can choose to watch a short advert for one extra life in that game. It's optional, and it's offered once per game. If the button doesn't appear, no advert was available at that moment or you had already used it.

When do adverts appear?

Never while you're flying. A full-screen advert may appear after some game overs, and a short advert plays if you choose "Keep going". Adverts are set to be suitable for all ages.

My best score has gone

Your best score is saved on your phone only. Deleting the game, or moving to a new phone without restoring a backup, starts it again from zero.

Does it work without the internet?

Yes. The game plays offline. Adverts and the "Keep going" option need a connection.

The game froze or closed

Close the game fully and open it again, and check the App Store for an update. If it keeps happening, email us with your iPhone model and iOS version.
