Santa's Chimney Hop

Privacy policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

In short: the game has no accounts and asks for no personal details. Your best score stays on your phone. The game shows adverts through Google AdMob, set to child-safe, non-personalised ads, and it never asks to track you.

Who we are

Santa's Chimney Hop ("the game") is published by Mitchell Reid, an individual developer based in the United Kingdom. For anything about this policy or your data, email mitchellreidd92@gmail.com.

What the game itself collects

Nothing that identifies you. The game does not ask for your name, email address, photos, contacts or precise location, and there is nothing to sign in to.

Two things are saved on your own device so the game works properly:

  • your best score
  • whether you have sound switched on or off

These never leave your phone, and they are removed if you delete the game.

Adverts

The game is free and is paid for by adverts supplied by Google AdMob. To deliver and measure adverts, Google's software inside the game may collect some technical information, including:

  • your device's IP address, which can indicate your approximate (not precise) location
  • device and app identifiers, and details such as device model, operating system and language
  • which adverts were shown and whether they were tapped
  • crash and performance information

We have set the game up so that:

  • all advert requests are marked as child-directed and for users under the age of consent
  • adverts are non-personalised, meaning they are not based on your past activity
  • adverts are limited to content rated suitable for general audiences
  • the game never asks for permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites, and does not do so

Google handles this information under its own policies. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

We do not receive information that identifies you from Google. We see overall totals only, such as how many adverts were shown.

Sharing your score

If you tap "Share score", your phone's own share menu opens with a short message containing your score. It is only sent if you choose an app and send it yourself. The game does not see who you share with.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and adverts are restricted as described above. If you are a parent or guardian and have a concern, email mitchellreidd92@gmail.com.

Legal basis and your rights (UK and EU)

Where data protection law applies, the limited processing described above is carried out on the basis of legitimate interests: providing a free game funded by non-personalised adverts, and keeping it working reliably.

You have the right to ask for access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data, and to object to or restrict its use. Because we hold no information that identifies you, we will usually point you to Google for data it holds; their tools are at myaccount.google.com. You can contact us at any time and we will help where we can.

If you are in the UK and are unhappy with how your data has been handled, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

How long data is kept

We keep no personal data ourselves. Your best score and sound setting stay on your device until you delete the game. Google keeps advert data for the periods set out in its own policy.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top.
